Meet H2 — the connected dash cam with a privacy you can feel. A compact 3-channel flagship that records the road, the cabin and the rear, stays on guard 24/7 over 4G, and gives you a physical privacy slide you control with one finger.

A privacy switch you can actually feel

One slide of the Hall-sensor cover physically blocks the lens and stops recording at the same time — no menus, no doubt. Choose On, Off, or Auto-Privacy (the cabin camera switches off by itself while parked). No mechanical wear, sensitive after tens of thousands of slides.

Always-on 4G sentinel

AOV (Always-on Video) keeps a continuous 24h watch while parked at up to 8× lower power than traditional time-lapse. If H2 detects an impact or a human shape, it switches to full-frame recording, saves a clip to the cloud, and pushes a real-time alert to your phone over 4G — wherever you are.

Built-in eSIM, online in ~3 minutes

No SIM tray, no waiting. H2 ships with an embedded eSIM — hold your phone near the device in the app and activate a data plan in about three minutes. Smart multi-network switching keeps the connection solid, even across borders.

AI clarity, day and night

A 1 TOPS NPU main processor with AI-ISP delivers smear-free low-light footage, 32× WDR for blinding tunnel exits and backlight, and next-gen smart encoding that beats H.265 compression by ~20% — sharper plates, smaller files.

Three cameras, full coverage

Front (F1.55, 136° wide), cabin (F2.0, 138°) and an optional rear (F2.0, 132°) — road, interior and behind you, all recorded together.

Engineered to never miss the moment

  • Supercapacitor + Seiko button battery — 3s of power after a sudden cut keeps the crash frame; accurate local timekeeping without relying on the network.
  • 5-system GNSS (GPS + GLONASS + Galileo + BDS + QZSS) with A-GPS, ~33s cold start.
  • Wi-Fi 6 dual-band (2.4/5.8 GHz, up to 35.85 MB/s) + Bluetooth quick-connect.
  • Pre-recording 10/20/30s buffers so the seconds before an incident are never lost.
  • Dual cooling — convection design + nano-carbon coating, rated −20°C to 70°C.
  • Adjustable bracket — 90° body + 13° front lens, tool-free to detach.

Frequently asked

Will it fit my car?

Yes for virtually any passenger vehicle. The mirror dash cam mounts over your factory rear-view mirror using two adjustable rubber straps and fits mirrors approximately 8 cm to 30 cm wide. Pickups, SUVs, sedans, RVs all work.

Do I need a hardwire kit?

Only if you want parking-monitor mode (camera records while the car is off). For normal driving recording, the included 12V cigarette-lighter cable is enough. The hardwire kit is sold separately and takes about an hour to install.

How large an SD card does it support?

Up to 256GB high-endurance microSD. A 64GB card is included in the box, pre-formatted. Always format new cards in-camera before use — never on a computer.

How long is the warranty?

2 years from the date of delivery, covering defects in materials and workmanship. We provide a prepaid return label for warranty claims and replacements ship within 2 business days.

What's the return window?

30 days from delivery, no questions asked. Unopened items: full refund. Opened: full refund less 10% restocking. Defective: full refund plus free return shipping.
