Questions

Before you buy.

Does H2 require a 4G subscription?

Remote live view, notices, and playback need an eligible plan. Driving recording and Wi-Fi work without one. Price, trial, and coverage are shown at activation.

How does the built-in eSIM work?

There is no SIM tray. A data plan is activated in the app. Coverage and plan terms still apply.

Can I use H2 without a 4G plan?

Yes for recording while you drive, and for Wi-Fi. Remote viewing and 4G notices wait until a plan is active.

How does AOV parking mode work?

AOV is a low-power watch after you park. It uses less power than time-lapse. On an impact or other supported activity, H2 switches to full-frame recording. It is not 24-hour 2.5K video.

Does parking mode require a hardwire kit?

Yes. Parking watch needs vehicle 12–24V through Type-C hardwire. The device runs at DC 5V 3A.

Can all three cameras record at once?

Yes. Front is 2.5K. Cabin and rear are 1080P. Each records at 30fps.

How does the privacy cover work?

Slide the cover to block the cabin lens. A Hall sensor reads the open or closed position.
